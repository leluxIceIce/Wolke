Wolke now lets you pick a companion for each SoundCloud widget on your Mac. Choose the sleeping cat, picnic bunny with corn and a basket, plague doctor and crow, mummy, witch-tower snail, or no mascot.

- Change the mascot on the desktop through **Edit Widget → Figur** in all three widget sizes.
- This update fixes the issue where macOS saved the selection but the desktop still showed the cat. The change from the edit menu was verified on a Mac without re-adding the widget.
- The widget shows your track and cover, with play and pause, previous and next track, 15-second seeking, likes, and access to SoundCloud’s playlist chooser.

Download **Wolke-1.8.1.zip** below. This beta runs on Apple silicon Macs with macOS 14 or later. Wolke uses your existing SoundCloud session in Safari. For installation and Safari extension setup, see the [README](https://github.com/leluxIceIce/Wolke#install).
